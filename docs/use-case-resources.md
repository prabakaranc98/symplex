# Configurable complex-system launch briefs

The six entries in [use_cases.json](../symplex/domains/use_cases.json) are editable investigation briefs. They supply a starting problem, intended estimate or decision, possible representations, evidence needs, validation obligations and visualization options. They contain no precomputed domain answer, fixed hypothesis or special-case solver. The agent must revise the brief with user context, choose the representation, acquire or admit evidence, write and execute models, compare actual results and report unresolved questions.

The six tracks are prediction markets, urban infrastructure, climate/agriculture/ecology, social simulation, cellular/enzymatic biology, and geopolitical/economic exposure. The present scope emphasizes modeling, prediction, estimation, comparison and optional exploratory intervention. Market creation, demand estimation, reinforcement learning, optimization and mechanism design are later extensions. Each case must preserve three separate evolving artifacts: the model of the system, any candidate intervention/solution, and the problem-solving method. A better numerical fit does not automatically establish a better intervention or a better general method.

Resource links below were checked against primary documentation on 2026-09-10. A resource being documented online does not mean Symplex has installed its package, connected its API or admitted its data. Current capability claims come from the application's registry and [recorded verification](verification.md): hosted Python, local table/retrieval/RDF tools and generic artifact visualizations exist; OpenMM, Mesa and QuantLib were unavailable in the recorded hosted probe, and advanced graph/map/3D integrations remain planned. Re-probe the chosen execution environment before relying on any optional library.

## Resource rationale and access boundaries

**Airport planning and access.** SUMO supplies a microscopic transport simulator; its maintained TraCI interface exposes Python control and observation of simulations. OSMnx is a potential network preparation and analysis tool. These provide useful extension surfaces, while candidate sites, demand calibration and feasibility evidence remain case-specific inputs. Neither package is an automatically validated airport-planning model. [SUMO documentation](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/), [TraCI Python interface](https://sumo.dlr.de/docs/TraCI/Interfacing_TraCI_from_Python.html), [OSMnx documentation](https://osmnx.readthedocs.io/en/stable/).

**Cellular responses and enzyme-deficiency research.** BioModels provides literature-based mechanistic models; its curation checks correspondence with the original publication. That scope differs from independent validation for a new biological question. COMBINE separates biological model representations, simulation experiments and their packaging. OpenMM is an optional molecular-simulation backend, requiring an appropriate scientific protocol and actual runtime support. The brief asks for model comparison and evidence gaps, not treatment advice or biological design instructions. [BioModels curation scope](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/online/courses/biomodels-quick-tour/what-is-biomodels/), [COMBINE standards](https://co.mbine.org/standards/), [OpenMM guide](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/userguide/).

**Prediction markets.** PyMC offers a flexible probabilistic modeling interface, useful when the agent can justify its forecast and observation models. World Bank indicators can supply economic context; they are not a point-in-time forecast archive. The engine's existing narrow P1 contract handles prepared threshold-event packs and host scoring; real inputs still need settlement definitions, availability times, dependent-event grouping and independent outcomes. Broader platform acquisition is a separate connector task. Market creation and demand estimation remain future extensions. [PyMC overview](https://www.pymc.io/projects/docs/en/stable/learn/core_notebooks/pymc_overview.html), [World Bank Indicators API](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api-documentation).

**Geopolitical and economic exposure.** UCDP exposes versioned conflict data and codebooks; its current API requires an access token requested from the maintainer. GDELT provides event/news-derived datasets and documentation. Preserve the distinction between reported mentions, independently coded events and latent developments. Duplicated reporting and retrospective information must not become artificial certainty. The brief concerns strategic political/economic/humanitarian analysis, without tactical military planning. [UCDP API](https://ucdp.uu.se/apidocs/), [GDELT data documentation](https://gdeltproject.org/data.html).

**Climate, agriculture and ecology.** The Hudson River is a concrete ecological/social example: HRECOS provides high-frequency water-quality and weather observations, live views and a data-request route; this is not an already integrated bulk API. USGS documents OGC water-data endpoints, STAC discovery and water-quality access. Xarray is a possible interface for labeled multidimensional arrays. An agricultural investigation needs its own soil, crop, management and climate inputs; Hudson data must not be substituted for them. All couplings need compatible scales and evidence. [HRECOS](https://hrecos.org/), [USGS Water Data APIs](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/docs/), [Xarray documentation](https://docs.xarray.dev/en/stable/).

**City population and social simulation.** Census ACS can supply aggregate social, housing and demographic estimates; its current API documentation requires a key for data queries. Mesa provides a Python agent-based modeling framework, while deck.gl offers a potential visualization layer for spatial and 3D results. These are separate responsibilities: behavioral validity is not established by an animated city scene. The current engine can preserve geometry and computed artifacts, but Mesa and advanced 3D rendering are capability gaps until implemented and tested. [Census ACS data](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/acs-5year.html), [Mesa documentation](https://mesa.readthedocs.io/stable/), [deck.gl documentation](https://deck.gl/docs).

All acquisitions should retain the exact source, retrieval time, version, license/access conditions, schema, units, time support and content digest. The evidence synthesist should distinguish source assertions, inferred relationships, assumptions and user judgments; preserve disagreement; and avoid counting copied or dependent sources as independent support. Every visualization should retain the basis of its displayed quantities: observed, derived, fitted or synthetic.
